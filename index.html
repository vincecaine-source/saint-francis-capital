How we invest

A disciplined, direct approach to investing

Investment products like mutual funds and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) can have a place in a portfolio. But the investments we manage for clients are primarily direct positions in individual stocks — selected, sized, and monitored with the same discipline Kaye brought to a flight plan.

We've made a significant investment in third-party research and analytical tools. That lets us run an exceptionally deep review of the portfolios we manage for clients — and it's the same toolkit we bring to a complimentary look at your existing portfolio, whether you work with us or not.
