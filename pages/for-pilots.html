What is the PRAP?

A plan built jointly by United and ALPA — administered through Schwab

The United Airlines Pilot Retirement Account Plan ("PRAP") is a custom defined-contribution retirement plan created jointly by United Airlines and the ALPA UAL MEC Retirement and Insurance Committee, exclusively for United Airlines pilots on the seniority list.

Saint Francis Capital is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by United Airlines, ALPA, or Charles Schwab. Plan details are provided for informational purposes only — always confirm current terms in your Summary Plan Description.

How to access your account

  • Log in through the Schwab Workplace Retirement App
  • Visit schwabplan.com/PRAP
  • Call the PRAP Service Center at 1-866-855-PRAP (1-866-855-7727)

What happens if you do nothing

New participants are auto-enrolled at a 5% pre-tax contribution rate after 30 days if they don't actively enroll. The default investment is a Target Date Fund aligned to the year you turn 65 — unless you choose otherwise. Passive defaults are a reasonable starting point, but they're rarely the optimal strategy for your specific timeline and goals.

Career-stage planning

The financial questions that come with flying for a living

These challenges are common to United, Southwest, Delta, and Spirit pilots and flight attendants alike — each with its own plan rules and its own recordkeeper (commonly Schwab for pilot plans, Fidelity for many flight attendant plans), but the same underlying planning questions.

01

Mandatory retirement at 65

FAA rules require airline pilots to retire at age 65. That creates a firm, known runway — and a bridging-income question for the years before Social Security and pension income fully phase in.

02

Furlough & leave-of-absence planning

Furloughs and extended leaves interrupt contributions and cash flow. A plan that anticipates these gaps holds up better than one that assumes uninterrupted service.

03

Pension & Social Security coordination

Where a pension exists, timing it against Social Security claiming decisions — and understanding any offsets — changes the math meaningfully.

04

Deferred compensation strategy

Deferred comp elections are often irrevocable once made. Sequencing them against your broader tax picture and retirement timeline matters.

05

Long-term disability analysis

A medical event that ends a flying career is a financial event too. Understanding your LTD coverage before you need it is part of a complete plan.

06

"Early-out" retirement program analysis

When airlines offer early-out packages, the decision window is short and the numbers are dense. We help evaluate the offer against your actual retirement plan, not just the headline figure.

07

PRAP contribution & rollover strategy

From adjusting your PRAP contribution rate above the 5% default to evaluating a rollover through the Schwab PCRA window, we help you make an active choice instead of accepting the default.
