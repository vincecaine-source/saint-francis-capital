About Saint Francis Capital

From the flight deck to the fiduciary's desk

An independent firm founded on the same discipline, preparation, and judgment that a 22.5-year airline career demands.

KR

Founder & President

Kaye Riggs

Kaye Riggs founded Saint Francis Capital in June 2010 after a 22.5-year career as an airline captain. She holds an Airline Transport Pilot certificate (737/757/767/CASA-212, FAA) and is a Series 65-licensed Investment Advisor Representative. Her approach to wealth management carries the same rigor she brought to the flight deck: a clear plan, disciplined execution, and a healthy respect for what's outside your control.

Each month, Kaye also writes a personal letter to Saint Francis Capital clients, sharing market perspective alongside reflections drawn from her flying career. Read the latest edition on our Insights page.

"We always sit on your side of the table." — Kaye Riggs, Founder & President

Independent & fiduciary

Not affiliated with any brokerage, fund company, or insurance conglomerate

Saint Francis Capital is an independent Registered Investment Advisor. We are not owned by, or affiliated with, any brokerage house, mutual fund company, insurance conglomerate, or financial institution. As fiduciaries, we are obligated to act in your best interest — full stop. It's not what you do to create your wealth, but how you manage it that determines the outcome, and the most important factor in any plan is the time you have remaining to act on it.

The name behind the firm

Why "Saint Francis"

Saint Francis of Assisi is remembered, among other things, for his gentleness toward animals — it's part of why this firm carries his name. Kaye has always kept a houseful of rescued dogs and cats, and that same soft spot extends into how the firm gives back: we support local animal rescue and shelter organizations, and a portion of what we give away each year goes to their work.

It's a small thing next to the work of managing a family's wealth, but it says something about how we operate — with patience, with care for the vulnerable, and without forgetting that the people (and pets) behind a portfolio are the reason any of it matters.
